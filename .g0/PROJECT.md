# ChalkTab

- Product ID: `G0-P002`
- Founder / Product Owner: Jelani Brice (`AgentBim`)
- G0 Core version / source / commit: `1.0.0` / `AgentBim/Codex-Agents` / `3ffccfc6eeec70efcc480443204850ee22bb1312`
- Product charter approval: Founder instruction in the Codex task on 2026-09-13: bootstrap ChalkTab as G0's second product; recorded in [ADR-001](decisions/ADR-001-adopt-ground-zero.md).
- Problem, audience, desired outcome: Give a single owner-coach or gym administrator a fast, trustworthy mobile ledger for adult class attendance, BBD pay-per-session charges, payments, balances, corrections, daily review, recurring sessions, and client account statements.
- In scope: One owner, one location, Barbados business dates, BBD integer-cent accounting, active and archived students, held-session charges, cash/transfer/other payments, immutable corrections, audit history, recurring sessions, daily review, and printable statements.
- Out of scope until separately approved: Multi-coach roles, multiple locations, memberships/packages, card processing, tax invoicing, automated client messaging, client accounts, accounting-system claims, and destructive ledger cleanup.
- Success metrics and baseline: Zero unexplained balance discrepancies; zero duplicate or lost writes; routine payment entry under 20 seconds; first held attendance and payment within 5 minutes of setup; at least 95% of active class days reviewed without unresolved scheduled sessions; pilot coaches reconcile held classes by end of day. Product analytics baseline is unknown; G0-12 owner must define a privacy-safe manual pilot baseline before instrumentation.
- Constraints, budgets, approved services: Existing Next.js, Supabase, Google OAuth, GitHub, and Vercel services are approved within their current plans. No new paid service or meaningful recurring cost is approved. Preview work must not expose production customer data. Package versions and lockfiles remain pinned.
- Data classification, privacy/security requirements: Student identity, attendance, balances, payment history, and statements are confidential operational and financial records. Authentication, owner-scoped RLS, server-side validated writes, immutable corrections, least privilege, and non-destructive rollback are mandatory. Credentials and secret values remain only in managed environment stores.
- UX principles and accessibility requirements: Mobile-first one-handed use; safe-area support; clear balance language; contextual errors that retain input; 44px primary touch targets; keyboard operation; visible focus; usable at 320px and 200% zoom; A4 and iPhone statement output without clipping.
